BindLens Web

Privacy

BindLens Web is a free program that runs in your web browser. It has no servers and no accounts of its own.

What BindLens Web can see

  • You sign in with your Google account. BindLens asks Google for one permission: drive.file. With it, BindLens can see and change only the files and folders it made (the "BindLens" folder in your Google Drive and what is in it) or the files you give it yourself with "Add from my Drive". It cannot see the rest of your Drive, your email or your contacts.
  • It reads your name and email address from your Google account to show you who is signed in.

Where your things are kept

  • Your books, documents and marks are saved in your Google Drive, in the BindLens folder.
  • The settings of your library (book colours and order, where you left off, reminders, statuses, signatures you drew) are in the file "BindLens library.json" in that same folder.
  • To open books fast, your browser keeps copies of your files and of the words read from them on your computer. "Clear the copies in this browser" in Settings takes them away. Signing out does not delete your Drive files.

What is not done

  • Your documents are not sent to the author of BindLens or to anybody else: they go from your Google Drive to your browser and back.
  • There is no advertising, no selling of data and no tracking inside the program.

Other services it uses

  • Google (sign-in and Google Drive), under Google's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages serves the program's files (like any website, it can see your IP address when the page loads).
  • Reading the words of a scanned page downloads the reading engine (Tesseract) from jsDelivr, a public network of files, the first time.
  • "Add to Google Calendar" opens Google Calendar with the date filled in; nothing is added until you save it there.
  • "Show it as it looks" (for Word, Excel and PowerPoint files) asks Google Drive to convert a copy of the file in your Drive; the copy is deleted straight after.

Your control

  • You can take BindLens's access away at any time in your Google Account: Security, Third-party apps and services.
  • You can delete the BindLens folder in your Drive at any time.
