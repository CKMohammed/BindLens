In short: BindLens works on your computer. It has no account, collects nothing about you and sends
nothing anywhere.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
  - Your documents stay where they are. BindLens reads them where they are and writes your marks
    (highlights, notes, stamps…) into your own PDF files, only when you make them.
  - Your library, settings, positions, reminders, calendar notes, the index for searching, the record of
    changes and your PIN (kept as a code that cannot be read back) are kept in %APPDATA%\BindLens on this
    computer.
  - Errors are written to BindLens-error.log next to BindLens. Nothing is sent to anyone.
  - Reports are made on your computer and saved only where you save them.

WHEN BINDLENS USES THE INTERNET (only when you ask)
  - Photos for the background or Home: Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) and Lorem Picsum
    (picsum.photos).
  - A calendar link you add (Outlook, Google…): the address you gave.
  - Reading scanned pages: the text-reading data is downloaded once from GitHub (github.com), if it is not
    on your computer yet.
  Those sites see your computer's internet address, as with any website you visit. BindLens sends them
  nothing about you or your documents.

  Email: "Send by email" opens your own email program with the file attached - BindLens does not send it.
  Office: Word, Excel and PowerPoint pages are made by Microsoft Office or LibreOffice on your computer.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
  If you keep other people's personal data in your documents (for example staff, children, clients), you
  remain responsible for it under data protection law (GDPR), as with any file on your computer. BindLens
  does not process it anywhere else. A PIN (File > Lock with a PIN) helps on a shared computer.

TO REMOVE EVERYTHING
  Delete the BindLens program folder and %APPDATA%\BindLens. Your own documents stay; marks you made
  stay inside your PDFs (BindLens can take them off before, if you want).
